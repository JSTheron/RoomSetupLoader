ROOM SETUP LOADER (RSL): ABOUT & DISCLAIMER
Version 1.41

Room Setup Loader is a Windows utility designed to load room setup still images from a Windows-based system, including Crestron Automate VX installations, into the media pool of a compatible Blackmagic Design ATEM video switcher.

The images can be used by an AV control system as visual positioning guides over a live camera feed, helping room users arrange tables and other furniture in predefined positions. The utility runs at Windows user logon to replenish still images that may be lost when the switcher restarts or loses power.

## Usage & Prerequisites
- `ATEM` Video Switchers 10.4.1 or 11.0.0 must be installed on the Automate VX.
- `CRESTRON` Automate VX 6.7 must be installed on the Automate VX.

## Configuration and installation
The settings of this application are set during the installer and can be modified later by re-running the installer or editing the config.json file located at %PROGRAMDATA\Room Setup Loader.

## Limitations
Images are pushed to the media pool on logon of a Windows user. The ATEM Media Pool will be not automatically be restored after an ATEM-only reboot whilst the Windows user remains logged on.

As the Automate VX has a built in nightly reboot, it is expected that the Media Pool will be pushed at least once a day.
